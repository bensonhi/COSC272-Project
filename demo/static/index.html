About this demo

Given a scientific claim and the abstract(s) it cites, the model answers SUPPORT, CONTRADICT or NOT ENOUGH INFO and scores every sentence as evidence. d1-3B answers one three-way question and one yes/no question per sentence in a single pass, without generating text.

How to use it

  1. Pick a claim: a suggested example, a search result, or a random one.
  2. Read the verdict on the right and the highlighted evidence sentences on the left.
  3. Remove the predicted or gold evidence and watch whether the model switches to NOT ENOUGH INFO. Switch models to compare.

Models

  • Zero-shot: d1-3B with no fine-tuning.
  • Original SFT: LoRA fine-tuned on the original training claims.
  • Augmented SFT: the same, plus copies of each claim with evidence sentences deleted (labelled NOT ENOUGH INFO when all evidence is gone).
  • Paired hinge: augmented training plus a loss that makes the true label beat NOT ENOUGH INFO by a wider margin on the full abstract than on the deleted one.

What you are seeing

Every verdict on this page was computed in advance by the real models on a GPU; nothing runs in your browser. For each claim you can view the abstract as given, with each model's predicted evidence removed (once or twice), with all gold evidence removed, or with any single sentence removed. Edited variants are LLM-rewritten test claims whose claim was negated, or whose evidence was reversed or made irrelevant.

Limitations

  • A research prototype and reading aid, not a substitute for reading the paper or for expert judgement. Probabilities can be confidently wrong.
  • Trained and tested on SciFact biomedical abstracts only. On other claim sources the models abstain far too often.
  • Gold rationales can miss valid evidence, so deleting them does not always leave a truly undecidable abstract.
